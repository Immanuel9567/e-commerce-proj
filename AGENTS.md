# Repository agent instructions

Read and follow [agent.md](agent.md), together with [README.md](README.md), [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) and [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md). The roadmap is planning-only; do not start a phase without the owner's approval.

Two rules apply to every step, not just large ones:

1. Record what was completed in the [roadmap progress log](ROADMAP.md#progress-log) before finishing a step. Logged work never ticks a roadmap phase.
2. End every completed step by asking the owner a short questionnaire about what to do next, and wait for the answer instead of continuing.
